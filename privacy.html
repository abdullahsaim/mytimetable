Privacy policyPolitique de confidentialité

Last updated 26 September 2026Dernière mise à jour : 26 septembre 2026

In short
  • Cadence stores your timetable only so it can work and sync across your devices.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies. Your data is never sold.
  • You can export or delete everything at any time in Settings, then Privacy and your data.

1. Who is responsible

Cadence is run by Hafiz Muhammad Abdullah, an independent developer based in France, who is the data controller. Contact: +33 7 45 46 39 57.

2. What we collect

  • Account: your email address, your password (stored only as a secure hash by our login provider) and, if you give it, your name.
  • Timetable: the events you add or import (title, type, date, times, place), calendar links you add, and which imported events you hid.
  • Settings: language, clock format, reminder and summary choices, work-hour limit, hourly pay rates and availability preferences.
  • Notification devices: if you turn on notifications, the push address your browser gives us, its encryption keys and your time zone, plus a record of which reminders were sent (kept 30 days) so you don't get duplicates.
  • Technical logs: our hosting providers keep standard security logs (such as IP address and time of request) for a short period.

We do not collect your location, contacts, payslips, documents, audio recordings, or any analytics about how you use the app.

3. Why, and on what legal basis

  • To provide Cadence (store, sync and display your timetable, calculate hours and pay): performance of our agreement with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To send notifications when the app is closed: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time by turning notifications off.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

4. Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts the database, login and server functions, in the Paris region.
  • GitHub serves the app files and sees your IP address when you load them.
  • Your browser's push service (Google for Chrome and Android, Apple for Safari and iPhone, Mozilla for Firefox) delivers notifications. Their content is end-to-end encrypted, so the push service cannot read it.
  • Voice input uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. In Chrome, audio is processed by Google; in Safari, by Apple. Cadence only ever receives the resulting text. You can always type instead.
  • Calendar import: when you add a university or work calendar link, our server fetches it from that website on your behalf.
  • WhatsApp is only involved if you choose to share your availability through it.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Where data leaves the EU, it is protected by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account, timetable and settings: for as long as you keep your account. When you delete your account, they are removed from the database immediately; provider backups are overwritten within a few days.
  • Notification send records: 30 days.
  • If you use Cadence without an account, your data stays only on your device and never reaches us.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, export, restrict or delete your data, object to its use, and withdraw consent at any time. The quickest way is in the app: Settings, then Privacy and your data, which has Export my data (a JSON file) and Delete my account. You can also email us; we reply within one month. If you believe your data is mishandled, you can complain to the CNIL at cnil.fr.

7. Storage on your device

Cadence saves your timetable, settings and sign-in session in your browser's local storage and keeps an offline copy of the app. These are strictly necessary for the app to work, so no cookie banner is needed. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Security

All connections use HTTPS. Each account can only read its own data, enforced by the database itself. Passwords are never stored in readable form, and notifications are encrypted end to end.

9. Age

Cadence is intended for people aged 15 and over.

10. Changes

If this policy changes in an important way, we'll tell you in the app before the change applies. The date at the top shows the latest version.
